# Accessory Lore Book Hooks V0

Accessories should make people and rooms readable. They tell the player who
owns access, who carries tools, who has rank, who travels, and who is hiding
something.

## Book Hooks

| Accessory | Book title | What the player learns | Asset detail unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| belt | The Belt Carries More Than Cloth | strap, buckle frame, tongue, keeper loop | attachment sockets for pouches and weapons |
| pouch | Small Purses, Large Stories | flap, drawstring, belt loop, stitch line | loot and pickpocket detail prompts |
| brooch | Pins of Rank and Weather | ring frame, terminal, pin, cloth socket | cloak/faction/rank inspection |
| signet ring | A Ring That Signs | seal face, shoulder, engraving | identity and document clues |
| pendant | What Hangs Close to the Heart | chain loop, symbol face, wear socket | ritual/faction clue prompts |
| key ring | The Teeth of a Key | bow, shank, bit, ward cut | access-shape clues |
| chatelaine | Keys at the Belt | belt hook, chain, split ring, tool socket | household/guild authority clues |
| satchel | The Messenger's Flap | flap, gusset, strap, content socket | delivery and hidden-item clues |
| scabbard | An Empty Sheath Still Accuses | throat, chape, hanger strap | missing-weapon clues |
| spectacles | Glass for Tired Eyes | lens rim, bridge, case, lens socket | scholar/library clue prompts |
| seal matrix | The Face That Writes in Wax | stamp face, handle, engraving | faction or forgery clues |
| waterskin | The Skin That Holds a Road | neck, stopper, seam, liquid state | survival/travel clues |

## Writing Rules

- Tie every word to a visible part or source field.
- Make status tier readable without exposition.
- Use accessories as evidence: ownership, access, travel, faction, wealth, or
  profession.
- Do not claim exact historical authenticity until a reference packet proves the
  specific variant.

## Example Inspect Lines

Key ring:

```text
The largest key has three deep ward cuts. It was made for a serious lock.
```

Brooch:

```text
The pin is newer than the ring frame, but the terminals still carry old polish.
```

Waterskin:

```text
The stopper is dry and the seam is swollen. It was emptied before it was dropped.
```
