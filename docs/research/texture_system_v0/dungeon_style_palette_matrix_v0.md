# Dungeon Style Palette Matrix V0

## Purpose

Dungeon style packs choose material mood, color, aging, and texture behavior.
They should be applied after geometry and component style sheets have named the
asset surfaces.

## Gothic Crypt

Base materials:

- cold limestone
- dark carved stone
- tarnished iron
- old wood
- candle wax

Surface behavior:

- black grime in recesses
- pale edge wear on bevels
- faint moss near floor contact
- soot under caps, arches, and torch zones
- dampness near floor and wall base

Decal policy:

- high tier: cracks, candle smoke, wall marks, localized moss
- low tier: no decals; use material masks and recess shadow slots

## Cathedral Ruin

Base materials:

- weathered limestone
- broken marble
- exposed dirt
- moss and lichen
- aged bronze

Surface behavior:

- chipped edges
- strong dirt in broken joints
- moss on horizontal ledges
- sun-bleached exposed faces
- darker undersides

Decal policy:

- high tier: cracks, ivy/moss patches, broken plaster stains
- low tier: no decals; use vertex colors and roughness variation

## Wet Sewer

Base materials:

Surface behavior:

- waterline staining

- glossy wet floors
- rust streaks below metal
- dark slime inside channels

Decal policy:

- high tier: slime splashes, leak streaks, rust streaks
- low tier: no decals; use wetness masks and material slots

## Lava Forge

Base materials:

- dark basalt
- charred stone
- black iron
- orange rust

Surface behavior:

- soot everywhere near heat sources
- orange glow in cracks

Decal policy:

- high tier: scorch marks, heat cracks, ash patches
- low tier: no decals; use emissive masks and procedural soot
