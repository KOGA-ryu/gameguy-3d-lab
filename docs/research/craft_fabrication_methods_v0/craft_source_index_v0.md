# Craft Source Index V0

This index records sources used for the fabrication-informed detail pass.

## Stone And Gothic Geometry

- Drawing Matter, "Medieval masons and tracing floors":
  https://drawingmatter.org/medieval-masons-and-tracing-floors/

  Repo use: full-scale construction drawing, selected lines, templates, and
  transfer logic for the drafting UI.

- Museum of the History of Science, "Medieval drawing":
  https://www.mhs.ox.ac.uk/geometry/content/cathedral.html

  Repo use: compass/straightedge geometry as practical construction vocabulary.

- Columbia Amiens, dynamic geometry:
  https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/amiens-arthum/dynamic-geometry

  Repo use: Gothic composition as geometric relationships rather than arbitrary
  ornament.

- Looking at Buildings, rib vaults:
  https://www.lookingatbuildings.org.uk/styles/medieval/roofs-and-vaults/stone-vaulting/rib-vaults.html

  Repo use: rib, web, boss, springing, and vault-bay vocabulary.

## Stone Carving And Masonry

- Regency Town House, stone carving:
  https://www.rth.org.uk/building-regency-houses/building-crafts-and-skills/stone-carving

  Repo use: staged carving tools such as point, claw, flat chisel, mallet, and
  banker bench.

- Nasher Museum, carving stone tools:
  https://nasher.duke.edu/stories/2019/02/carving-stone-tools/

  Repo use: tool-mark vocabulary and rough-to-finish carving sequence.

- Brick Industry Association Technical Notes 31, brick masonry arches:
  https://gobrick.com/media/file/31-Technical-Notes-31-Brick-Masonry-Arches.pdf

  Repo use: centering, voussoirs, keystone, springline, arch geometry, and
  temporary support logic.

## Wood Joinery And Moulding

- Common Woodworking, mortise and tenon:
  https://commonwoodworking.com/mortise-and-tenon/

  Repo use: mortise, tenon, shoulder, marking, and fitting vocabulary.

- Woodworkers Institute, frame and panel doors:
  https://woodworkersinstitute.com/techniques/2022/01/frame-panel-doors/

  Repo use: stiles, rails, panels, grooves, and frame logic.

- Encyclopaedia Britannica, plane tool:
  https://www.britannica.com/technology/plane-tool

  Repo use: hand-plane vocabulary and profile-cutting mental model.

- Woodworkers Institute, moulding planes:
  https://woodworkersinstitute.com/techniques/2022/02/moulding-planes/

  Repo use: profile moulding as a sequence of flats, rebates, coves, rounds,
  beads, and finishing passes.

## Ironwork And Metal Relief

- The Wroughtworks resources:
  https://www.thewroughtworks.com/resources

  Repo use: traditional forged ironwork process vocabulary for scrollwork,
  repeated bars, collars, and joins.

- National Park Service Preservation Brief 27, architectural metal:
  https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-27-architectural-metal.pdf

  Repo use: architectural metal component awareness and historic ornamental
  metal preservation vocabulary.

- Victoria and Albert Museum, metalworking techniques:
  https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/metalworking-techniques

  Repo use: repoussé/chasing/raised relief vocabulary for metal panels,
  hardware, and armor-like details.
