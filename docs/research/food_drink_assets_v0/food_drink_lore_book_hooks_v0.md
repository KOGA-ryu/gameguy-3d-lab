# Food And Drink Lore Book Hooks V0

Food and drink should make rooms feel lived-in. They show rank, hunger, trade,
travel, worship, neglect, and time.

## Writing Rules

- Tie every term to a visible part or source field.
- Use state words carefully: full, empty, cut, stale, rotten, sealed, broken.
- Reward reading with better room-history, trade, survival, poison, or ritual
  clues.
- Do not claim nutrition, recipe correctness, or food safety.

## Example Inspect Lines

Goblet:

```text
The cup is clean, but the liquid line is dark where the wine sat too long.
```

Grain sack:

```text
The cord is still tight. Whoever opened the storehouse chose a different sack.
```

Spoiled fruit:

```text
The fruit has collapsed inward. This room waited longer than its owners did.
```
