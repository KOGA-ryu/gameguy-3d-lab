# Source Quality Notes v0

Research Dex should stay in the measurement/source lane. It should produce source-backed measurement records only, not architecture research essays, Pattern Lab studies, Blender work, or visual inspiration boards.

## Best Source Types

- HABS/HAER/HALS measured drawings from the Library of Congress.
- Historic England measured drawings and archive records when dimensions or scale are usable.
- Project Gutenberg architectural treatises with explicit rules/proportions.
- Smithsonian digital architectural books.
- Internet Archive public-domain architectural pattern books.
- Cathedral/church official history pages only when they provide dimensions.
- UNESCO, museum, and heritage site pages only when they provide dimensions.
- Academic PDFs only when they include explicit measured diagrams or tables.

## Avoid

- Beautiful images with no dimensions.
- Pinterest, AI images, tourist photos, and sources with unclear provenance.
- Cultural authenticity claims.
- Structural safety claims.
- Fabrication claims.
- Full building history unless it gives useful measurements.
- Too many examples of the same primitive before coverage is broad.

## Hard Rule

If a source has beautiful drawings but no usable dimensions or scalable proportions, record it as inspiration-only and do not extract measurements from it.
