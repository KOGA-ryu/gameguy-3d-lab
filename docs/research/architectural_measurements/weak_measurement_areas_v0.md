# Weak Measurement Areas v0

This file tracks missing evidence before source measurements can become reliable procedural grammar parameters. These are not asset blockers; they are evidence gaps.

## Weak Terms

- `wall_thickness_to_span`: no trustworthy v0 extraction. Keep null until a measured drawing or written source gives both values.
- `pier_to_span`: no full bay support-width-to-span record yet.
- `round_arch_bay`: metric span examples exist, but real-building rise/springline/frame-thickness evidence is weak.
- `vault_rib_cell`: v0 has envelope proportions, not rib section radii or rib thicknesses.
- `octagonal_plan_core`: plan dimensions exist, but connector bay and wall thickness relationships need measured examples.
- `column_base_shaft_capital`: column height/diameter ratios exist, but base/capital subdivision needs measured extraction.
- `horseshoe_arch_bay`, `ogee_arch_bay`, `buttress_profile`, `stair_tower`, `portal_frame`, `window_tracery_bay`, `blind_arcade`, and `cloister_bay`: fetch needed.

## Handling Rule

Do not fill gaps with visual guesses. Use nulls, proportional constraints, or `fetch_needed` status until a source gives a measured value, a scale-backed drawing, or an explicit treatise rule.
