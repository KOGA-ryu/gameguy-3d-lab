# Instrument Lore Book Hooks V0

These hooks are for game depth. Reading a book should teach the player useful
object vocabulary and make nearby props feel less generic.

## Book Hooks

| Instrument | Book title | What the player learns | Asset detail unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lute | The Luthier's Bowl | ribs, rose, bridge, pegbox | inspect prompts can name rib count and soundhole style |
| Gothic harp | The Harp Maker's Triangle | frame, soundbox, pillar, neck, string fan | player can distinguish harp frame damage from broken strings |
| recorder | The Split Breath | windway, labium, bore, tone holes | player can identify why blocked holes or damaged mouthpieces matter |
| shawm | A Reed That Carries | reed, staple, conical bore, flared bell | player can separate loud ceremonial reeds from simple flutes |
| frame drum | Skin Over Hoop | hoop, membrane, tension points, rim lips | player can notice loose tacks, torn skins, or missing jingles |
| cast bell | The Bell Founder's Waist | crown, shoulder, waist, soundbow, lip, clapper | player can inspect cracked bells or missing clappers |
| portative organ | On Bellows and Pipes | bellows, wind chest, pipes, pipe mouths, keys | player can repair bellows, blocked pipes, or missing keys |
| hurdy-gurdy | The Wheel That Bows | wheel, crank, keybox, tangents, drones, melody strings | player can understand crank/wheel/string repair tasks |

## Writing Rules

- Use craft terms after the player has seen the object.
- Tie the term to a visible part on the asset.
- Reward reading with better inspection text, repair hints, barter value, or
  hidden-object recognition.
- Do not claim perfect historical accuracy unless a later reference packet
  proves the exact variant.

## Example Inspect Lines

Lute:

```text
The rose is chipped, but the ribs of the bowl are still tight.
```

Recorder:

```text
The labium edge is cracked. This will split the breath before it can sing.
```

Bell:

```text
The clapper hangs crooked, and the soundbow is bright from old strikes.
```

Hurdy-gurdy:

```text
The wheel is dry and pale where rosin should darken its rim.
```
