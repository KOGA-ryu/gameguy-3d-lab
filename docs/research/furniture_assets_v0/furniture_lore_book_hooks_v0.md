# Furniture Lore Book Hooks V0

Furniture should teach room hierarchy. A player should be able to read a room by
its furniture even before opening a UI prompt.

## Book Hooks

| Furniture | Book title | What the player learns | Asset detail unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rough plank stool | A Stool is Never Just a Stool | plank, leg, stretcher, peg, repair patch | common clutter and breakable props can be inspected by part |
| joined bench | The Long Seat of the Hall | apron, stretcher, peg, wear lane | hall/refectory benches reveal traffic and seating order |
| boarded chest | Straps, Locks, and Secrets | lid, strap, hinge, lock plate, coffer | loot containers can reveal security and owner status |
| trestle table | The Board and the Trestle | trestle, board, stretcher, peg hole | hall tables can mark rank, crowd size, and temporary use |
| panelled cupboard | The Cupboard Learns to Stand | carcass, shelf, raised panel, cornice | storage can identify merchant, church, or scholarly rooms |
| high-back chair | The Back of Rank | high back, arms, cushion, back posts | seating height and comfort reveal status |
| canopy bed | Curtains Around Sleep | canopy, post, curtain, headboard, mattress | bedroom furniture can signal wealth, privacy, and warmth |
| lectern | The Desk That Faces Upward | sloped top, book stop, side panel, book socket | reading/scribing furniture can become puzzle stations |
| workbench | A Bench is a Map of Work | bench top, peg row, tool slots, vise hint | crafting rooms reveal trade and missing tools |
| throne | The Seat that Makes a Room Kneel | central axis, crest, symbol panel, dais socket | authority spaces can identify faction and ritual rank |

## Writing Rules

- Tie every term to a visible part.
- Use furniture caste tier to decide room flavor.
- Reward reading with better inspect text, repair clues, theft value, puzzle
  hints, or room-history flavor.
- Do not claim exact historical accuracy unless a future reference packet proves
  the specific variant.

## Example Inspect Lines

Chest:

```text
The front strap is newer than the hinge, and the lock plate has been replaced.
```

Bench:

```text
The center of the seat is polished smooth; this bench has served a hall for years.
```

Throne:

```text
The crest was carved after the chair was built. Someone changed the owner, not the seat.
```
